# GPT Image 2.6 vs 2.5: What Changed, and Should You Upgrade?

![GPT Image 2.6 vs 2.5: What Changed, and Should You Upgrade?](https://resource.cometapi.com/console/blog-banner/2026/11/0fe8ef841f784c70ab2735ff49f6eb07.png)

## TL;DR

GPT Image 2.6 is the more flexible upgrade for teams that need faster image creation, tighter editing control, or GIF and SVG workflows. Released on November 4, 2026, it retains the two-model lineup: Flare for responsive creation and Sunburst for detailed work. Its clearest additions are independent planning control and new output workflows; standard token rates remain the same as 2.5.

If you already use GPT Image 2.5, start with the corresponding 2.6 variant and your existing prompts. Upgrade when it improves the work you deliver: faster previews, fewer corrections, or an output format you previously had to build separately. Keep 2.5 in the comparison for text-heavy assets, where the Arena results below still favor its current entries.

## Key Takeaways

- Compare Flare against Flare and Sunburst against Sunburst before drawing conclusions about either generation.
- OpenAI reports faster generation in 2.6, improved layouts in Flare, and stronger edit consistency in Sunburst at medium or high quality.
