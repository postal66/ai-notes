## What is the Official Price of ChatGPT Plus in Russia? 💳

OpenAI’s publicly stated price for ChatGPT Plus remains **USD $20 per month** (billed monthly). This is the baseline global list price OpenAI advertises for Plus. ### Is ChatGPT Plus available in Russia ?

No. OpenAI’s published list of **supported countries** does **not** include Russia, and multiple reports confirm OpenAI is not supporting accounts in certain countries, Russia among them. That means there is **no official, OpenAI-managed, Ruble-priced ChatGPT Plus subscription for resident users in Russia** at the time of writing.

**Implication:** If you are physically in Russia and want to subscribe to ChatGPT Plus, there is no fully supported path guaranteed by OpenAI. Attempting to use geo-workarounds or foreign payment methods may violate OpenAI’s terms, local rules, or both — and can result in blocked accounts or payment failures.

## Why Is the Price Different Across Various Payment Methods?

In markets where OpenAI **does** accept local billing, prices can be localized (billed in local currency through local partners or app stores). Because OpenAI does **not** officially support Russia, you should expect:

- App store billing (Apple/Google) to be unreliable in Russia due to prior billing suspensions and because some apps are removed or unavailable in the Russian app stores.
- Banks and payment providers to apply FX spreads and possible sanctions-related restrictions that can make cross-border purchases harder. Russian residents may face additional friction or blocking when trying to use foreign payment rails.

**Bottom line:** If you could pay USD $20 from Russia, the *all-in* cost will be the USD→RUB conversion + any card/FX markup + any applicable VAT or taxes. That total commonly pushes the cash cost meaningfully above the mid-market conversion number.

Because direct billing is blocked for Russian cards, savvy users in 2026 have moved toward more complex financial routes to keep their AI access active.

### The Virtual Card Route (Old Reliable)

Many users use virtual cards from neighboring regions like Kazakhstan or Turkey. While the $20 price is honored by OpenAI, the user pays a "convenience fee" to the service providing the card. This remains the most stable way to maintain an official Plus account.

### Third-Party "Account Top-ups"

Marketplaces like Plati or G2A offer "gift" activations. Here, the price is fixed in Rubles but includes a heavy markup. In early 2026, these services often list ChatGPT Plus at **3,500 RUB**, providing a "one-click" solution for those without access to foreign banking.

### The App Store Workaround

For users with Apple IDs set to other regions (like the US or Turkey), subscribing through the iOS app remains a popular choice. This avoids the need for a web-based credit card but requires the purchase of region-specific App Store gift cards, which also carry a premium.

### Other Methods

- Using a foreign Apple ID / Google Play account or a foreign payment card to buy Plus via the mobile app.
- Subscribing via a foreign Stripe/billing checkout (requires that OpenAI accept the country on the card and the user’s account isn’t blocked).
- Using VPNs or other geo-obfuscation to appear outside Russia.
