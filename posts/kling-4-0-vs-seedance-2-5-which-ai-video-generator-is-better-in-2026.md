# Kling 4.0 vs Seedance 2.5: Which AI Video Generator Is Better in 2026?

![Kling 4.0 vs Seedance 2.5: Which AI Video Generator Is Better in 2026?](https://resource.cometapi.com/console/blog-banner/2026/11/fe17f4f47d4446369375a59de8053072.png)

## TL;DR

Kling 4.0 and Seedance 2.5 generate video with synchronized audio, but they suit different production briefs. Start with Kling 4.0 when your storyboard requires explicit keyframes or prescribed camera moves. Consider Seedance 2.5 when you need a larger reference set, direct audio references, or timestamp-based audio-video editing. Both models support targeted video edits.

Updated November 5, 2026, this comparison covers official capabilities, published tests from CellCog and Fuser, and pricing from fal and CometAPI. Platform-specific settings are identified where they affect access, output, or cost.

## Key Takeaways

- **Length and sound:** Kling 4.0 and Seedance 2.5 both support up to 30 seconds per generation with audio.
- **Creative inputs:** Kling offers up to 10 keyframes and 15 combined reference assets; Seedance supports up to 50 multimodal references. Input capacity alone does not establish output quality.
