# OpenAI releases ChatGPT Agent with pleasure

![OpenAI releases ChatGPT Agent with pleasure](https://resource.cometapi.com/blog/uploads/2025/03/chatgpt.jpg)

OpenAI officially unveiled its latest advancement in AI-driven productivity: the ChatGPT Agent. This new feature transforms ChatGPT from a conversational assistant into a proactive digital agent capable of autonomously carrying out complex, multi-step tasks on behalf of users. The announcement, made during a livestream featuring CEO Sam Altman, positions the ChatGPT Agent as a significant leap forward in AI utility and marks a pivotal moment in the evolution of intelligent assistants.

## Key Capabilities

The ChatGPT Agent integrates a “virtual computer” environment, allowing it to reason, plan, and execute tasks across web interfaces, documents, and third‑party services without manual intervention. Core functionalities include:

- **Web Browsing & Research**: Conducts in-depth research across public websites and uploaded files, gathering and synthesizing information.
- **Form Filling & Transactions**: Completes online forms, places orders, and manages bookings (e.g., flight reservations, dinner reservations) with user‑approved actions.
- **Document & Slide Generation**: Generates editable PowerPoint presentations and Excel spreadsheets, enabling users to obtain fully‑formatted deliverables directly from the agent .

---

## How It Works

Users can activate the ChatGPT Agent via an “agent mode” toggle in the ChatGPT interface’s tools dropdown. Once enabled, the agent narrates its actions in real time, offering transparency into every step of the process. At any point, users retain full control—permissions are explicitly requested before any consequential action (e.g., sending emails, making purchases), and tasks can be interrupted or redirected mid‑operation .

The feature leverages “connectors” to interface with user accounts and third‑party applications. While connectors streamline workflows (such as calendar management or inbox summarization), manual login prompts ensure robust security and data privacy safeguards .

---

## Evolution from Operator and Deep Research

The ChatGPT Agent builds upon OpenAI’s earlier toolsets—Operator and Deep Research—by merging their strengths into a unified platform. Operator’s browser‑based automation has been subsumed into the Agent’s virtual browser, while Deep Research’s autonomous web‑sourced analysis is now a seamless part of the agent’s toolkit. As Operator’s standalone interface is deprecated in the coming weeks, users will transition to the more versatile Agent environment for all agentic tasks .

---

## Availability and Access

ChatGPT Agent is initially available to subscribers on the **Pro**, **Plus**, and **Team** tiers of ChatGPT. Activation is as simple as selecting “agent mode” from the tools dropdown or entering the `/agent` command within any chat session. Rollout to Plus and Team users may be staggered over several days, and full availability in the European Economic Area (EEA) and Switzerland is anticipated later in the summer of 2025. Enterprise and Education customers can expect access in the near future as part of OpenAI’s phased deployment strategy .

---

## Safety and Privacy Measures

Addressing concerns over autonomous AI actions, OpenAI has embedded multiple safety layers within the ChatGPT Agent:

- **Explicit Consent**: Any action impacting real‑world systems prompts a clear permission request.
- **Interruptibility**: Users can halt or override tasks at any stage, ensuring alignment with their objectives.
- **Risk Flagging**: The system flags potentially hazardous or unethical requests, pausing execution and notifying users for confirmation .

These measures underscore OpenAI’s commitment to responsible AI deployment and aim to mitigate errors or misuse in complex workflows.
