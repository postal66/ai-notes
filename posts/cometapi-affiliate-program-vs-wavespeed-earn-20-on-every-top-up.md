# CometAPI Affiliate Program vs WaveSpeed: Earn 20% on Every Top-Up

![CometAPI Affiliate Program vs WaveSpeed: Earn 20% on Every Top-Up](https://resource.cometapi.com/7029d84-f54e-4c67-a6b8-5369fd9fdd28.png)

The [CometAPI Affiliate Program](https://cometapi.tolt.io/) pays a 20% commission every time a referred customer tops up their CometAPI account. By comparison, the official WaveSpeed affiliate page advertises a 10% commission on eligible payments from referred users. For publishers, AI educators, developer communities, agencies, and technical creators, that headline difference is simple: on the same eligible $100 payment, the stated commission is $20 with CometAPI versus $10 with WaveSpeed, before any program-specific limits or validation rules are applied.

The percentage is not the only detail that matters. A responsible comparison should also examine what is being promoted, how long the referral cookie lasts, when payouts are processed, and how performance is tracked. This guide compares the two programs, explains the relevant terms, and shows where CometAPI may offer stronger earning potential.

> **Quick answer:**
>
> CometAPI pays 20% on every tracked top-up by a referred customer, offers a 120-day referral cookie, has a $50 payout threshold, follows a NET-15 payout schedule, and does not impose a cumulative commission cap. WaveSpeed advertises a 10% commission and a 30-day referral cookie. For affiliates, CometAPI combines twice the headline commission rate with a referral window that is four times as long.

## What Is CometAPI?

CometAPI is a unified AI API platform that provides access to more than 500 AI models through one API. Its official site positions the service as an OpenAI-compatible layer for text, image, video, audio, and embedding workflows. Instead of maintaining separate provider credentials and billing relationships, developers can use a single CometAPI key and change models with less integration work.

The platform uses pay-as-you-go credits rather than a mandatory monthly subscription. That matters to an affiliate audience because the referral proposition is concrete: introduce builders to a multi-model API, and earn a percentage when a referred customer adds funds to use it.

CometAPI's public website highlights several product characteristics that affiliates can explain without resorting to vague promotional language:

- Access to 500+ AI models through one platform
- OpenAI-compatible API integration
- Pay-as-you-go usage with no monthly fee
- Coverage across text, image, video, audio, and embedding models
- A dashboard for usage and cost monitoring
- Model switching without maintaining a separate key for every provider

These points give technical publishers several useful content angles: integration tutorials, model comparisons, API migration guides, workflow demonstrations, cost experiments, and practical AI automation examples.

## What Is WaveSpeed AI?

WaveSpeed AI is an AI media generation platform and API service. Its official homepage emphasizes image and video generation while also listing audio, 3D, avatar, and language models. The service targets both developers using APIs and creators using web or desktop tools.

WaveSpeed says its platform includes more than 1,000 models. Its model catalog and positioning make it especially relevant to creators and developers working with generative media. This is important context: WaveSpeed is a legitimate alternative to evaluate on product fit, not merely a commission percentage used in isolation.

Both platforms aggregate access to multiple AI models, but their public positioning differs:

- **CometAPI** presents itself as a unified API layer spanning a broad set of production AI workloads.
- **WaveSpeed AI** leads with AI media generation, especially image and video creation, while also supporting other model categories.

The better product recommendation depends on the audience. The more attractive affiliate structure depends on the program terms and the customer's expected spending pattern.

## How the CometAPI Affiliate Program Works

The CometAPI program follows three steps:

1. **Join through the affiliate portal.** Applications and affiliate accounts are handled through CometAPI's Tolt partner portal.
2. **Get a unique referral link.** The link can be used in tutorials, comparisons, demos, newsletters, communities, social posts, and other useful resources for AI builders.
3. **Earn when referred customers top up.** CometAPI states that affiliates receive a 20% commission every time a referred customer adds funds to an account.

The Tolt-powered dashboard displays clicks, conversions, and rewards. This gives affiliates a way to distinguish traffic from actual referred activity and to evaluate which content earns qualified conversions.

The referral cookie lasts **120 days**. In practical terms, a visitor who clicks a valid affiliate link has a substantially longer period in which a qualifying referral can be attributed than under a 30-day cookie. Confirmed commission becomes eligible for withdrawal after reaching **$50**, and payouts follow a **NET-15** schedule. CometAPI does not set a cumulative commission cap, so the program does not impose a fixed ceiling on total commission earnings.

CometAPI describes the program as suitable for creators, developers, educators, communities, agencies, and people with an audience interested in building with AI. In practice, a small but highly relevant technical audience may be more valuable than a large, general audience. A tutorial that solves a real integration problem can continue attracting qualified API users long after publication.

## How the WaveSpeed Affiliate Program Works

WaveSpeed also manages its program through Tolt. According to the official WaveSpeed affiliate page, an affiliate receives a unique link, shares it with an audience, and tracks activity through an affiliate dashboard.

WaveSpeed advertises a 10% commission. Its public page provides several additional rules:

- The referral cookie lasts 30 days.
- A registration completed within 30 days of the referral click is attributed to the affiliate.
- The program describes commissionable payments as payments made during a new user's first 30 days after registering through the affiliate link.
- There is no minimum payout threshold for a confirmed commission balance.
- Payouts follow a NET-15 schedule. For example, commission earned in April is processed around May 15.

These details make the WaveSpeed program relatively easy to understand. Its shorter referral window and lower commission rate are the most relevant distinctions for affiliates comparing it with CometAPI.
