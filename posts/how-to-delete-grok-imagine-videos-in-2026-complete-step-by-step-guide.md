## Step-by-Step: How to Delete Grok Imagine Videos (Web, App & API)

### 1. Deleting Individual Videos via the Imagine Gallery (Easiest Method)

**On Web (grok.com):**

1. Log into grok.com or X.com and open Grok.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Imagine** (or navigate to your generation history).
3. Scroll or search for the video. Generated clips appear as thumbnails with play icons and are often hearted (favorited) by default.
4. Hover over the thumbnail. You’ll see a heart icon and three-dot menu.
5. Click the **heart** to unsave/remove from favorites. A warning pops up: “You won’t be able to view this content again.” Confirm.
6. If the option appears, click the three dots > **Delete Post** > confirm deletion.
7. Refresh the page—the video should disappear instantly from your view.

**On Mobile App (iOS/Android):**

1. Open the Grok or X app and tap the Grok icon.
2. Tap the **Imagine** tab (bottom or side menu).
3. Tap the video thumbnail.
4. Tap the heart icon (or “More”/three dots).
5. Select **Unsave** or **Delete Post** and confirm. The same irreversible warning appears.

**Pro Tip:** Videos saved as “Posts” (if you shared them publicly) may require an extra “Delete Post” step after unsaving.way disappear from your view immediately but may remain in xAI’s systems for up to 30 days.

### 2. Bulk Deletion: Clear All Imagine Media at Once

For a full spring-clean:

1. Go to **Settings** (profile icon) → **Data Controls**.
2. Find **“Delete all Imagine media”** or **“Delete all conversations”** (this removes associated videos).
3. Click **Delete** and confirm.

This is the fastest way to wipe your entire Grok Imagine history.

### 3. Deleting via Conversation History

Since many videos are generated inside chats:

1. Go to conversation history.
2. Tap/click the specific chat → trash icon or “Delete Conversation.”
3. This automatically removes linked videos.

### 4. API Users (grok-imagine-video Endpoint)

If you generate via the official xAI API (e.g., client.video.generate with model="grok-imagine-video"):

- The response returns a temporary URL.
- **You control deletion**: Download the video immediately, then the URL expires naturally.
- No persistent gallery exists unless you save the URL yourself.
- For privacy, avoid storing prompts in your own logs and delete local files when done.

### 5. Advanced: Permanent Account-Level Deletion

For complete removal:

- **Delete your Grok account**: Settings → Account → Delete Account.
- **Submit a formal data deletion request**: Visit <https://x.ai/privacy-portal/> with your full name and email. xAI must process within legal timelines (typically 30 days).

### Troubleshooting Common 2026 Issues

- “Delete option grayed out”: Ensure you’re on the latest app version; older builds limit deletion.
- Video still visible after unsaving: Force-refresh or log out/in. Some users report a 5–10 minute sync delay.
- No “Delete Post” button: Try unsaving the heart first—many tutorials confirm this triggers full removal.
- Free-tier users locked out: Deletion still works, but you can’t regenerate similar videos without SuperGrok.
- Error “Content cannot be removed”: Rare server-side glitch reported ; contact @xai support via X or wait 24 hours.
