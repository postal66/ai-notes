## What Plans Does Runway AI Offer in 2025?

Runway AI offers five main plans as of mid-2025: Basic (Free), Standard, Pro, Unlimited, and Enterprise. These tiers are intentionally structured to grow with users, from those merely exploring AI tools to organizations demanding high-volume, enterprise-grade capabilities. It is crucial to note that all pricing details have been corroborated by sources updated as of April and May 2025.

### Basic (Free) Plan

- **Cost:** $0 (forever)
- **Credits:** One-time allocation of 125 credits
- **Video Generation:** Gen-1 (video-to-video) up to 4 seconds and Gen-2 (text/image-to-video) up to 16 seconds via Extend Video
- **Projects & Storage:** Up to 3 video projects and 5 GB of asset storage
- **Exports:** Video editor exports capped at 720p; limited image export options

The Basic plan is ideal for users who want to trial Runway AI’s core features without financial commitment. It provides a modest allocation of credits, enabling minimal experimentation with short video generations and simple edits .

### Standard Plan

- **Cost:** $15 per user per month (billed monthly) or $12 per user per month (billed annually at $144)
- **Credits:** 625 credits per month (roughly equivalent to 125 seconds of Gen-2 video or 125 image generations)
- **Video Generation:** Gen-1 up to 15 seconds; Gen-2 up to 16 seconds via Extend Video; Gen-3 Alpha (text/image-to-video) up to 10 seconds
- **Upscaling & Watermarks:** Ability to upscale resolution in Gen-1/Gen-2 and remove watermarks across Gen-1, Gen-2, and Gen-3 Alpha
- **Projects & Storage:** Unlimited video editor projects and 100 GB of asset storage
- **Exports:** 4K video editor exports, green screen alpha matte, 2K image exports, full 3D texture options; one custom AI generator training included

This mid-tier plan caters to individuals and small teams requiring consistent credit access, high-resolution outputs, and limited custom AI training. Many content creators prefer Standard for its balanced feature set relative to price .

### Pro Plan

- **Cost:** $35 per user per month (billed monthly) or $28 per user per month (billed annually at $336)
- **Credits:** 2,250 credits per month (approximately 450 seconds of Gen-2 video or 450 image generations)
- **Video Generation:** All Standard Plan capabilities for Gen-1, Gen-2, and Gen-3 Alpha with extended usage limits
- **Upscaling & Watermarks:** Included for Gen-1, Gen-2, and Gen-3 Alpha
- **Projects & Storage:** Unlimited video editor projects and 500 GB of asset storage
- **Exports:** All Standard Plan exports plus PNG & ProRes for video editor compositions and image exports
- **Custom AI & Voices:** One custom AI generator training included; ability to create custom voices for lip sync and text-to-speech

The Pro plan is targeted at serious creators and growing teams that require extensive credit allotments, advanced export formats, and additional storage. It also introduces advanced audio features, making it suitable for multimedia-intensive workflows .

### Unlimited Plan

- **Cost:** $95 per user per month (billed monthly) or $76 per user per month (billed annually at $912)
- **Credits:** Unlimited text-to-video (Gen-1, Gen-2, Gen-3 Alpha) generations in Explore Mode at relaxed rates; 2,250 credits per month in Credit Mode
- **All Pro Plan Features:** Includes everything in Pro, with no generation limits in Explore Mode
- **Use Case:** High-volume, nonstop content creators who require maximum freedom without worrying about monthly credit caps

Unlimited is designed for power users and agencies producing large quantities of AI-generated content. By removing generation caps in Explore Mode, it ensures production workflows remain uninterrupted, even at scale.

### Enterprise Plan

- **Cost:** Custom pricing (contact Runway for a tailored quote)
- **Included Features:** All Pro Plan functionalities plus:
- Single sign-on (SSO)
- Custom credit allocations and overage policies
- Custom storage capacities
- Model customizations (white-label or bespoke AI models)
- Configurable teamspaces to segment and organize large teams
- Advanced security and compliance (SOC 2, GDPR, etc.)
- Enterprise-wide onboarding and ongoing success program
- Priority support with dedicated account management
- Integration with internal tools (e.g., data lakes, CI/CD pipelines)
- Workspace analytics and reporting dashboards

Enterprise is aimed at large organizations needing robust security, administrative controls, dedicated support, and comprehensive customization. Pricing is determined based on usage needs, seat counts, and service-level agreements. Interested parties typically schedule a demo to finalize details.

## How Are Credits and Features Allocated Across Plans?

Runway’s credit system is central to its pricing model, determining how much AI-generated content a user can produce each month. Credits are consumed based on the length and type of content generated (e.g., Gen-1 vs. Gen-3) and on image generations. In addition to credits, feature access—such as watermark removal, upscaling, and export options—differentiates each plan. Understanding this allocation helps users select the optimal tier for their workflow needs.

### Credit Allotment by Plan

- **Basic:** One-time 125 credits; no monthly replenishment
- **Standard:** 625 credits per month
- **Pro:** 2,250 credits per month
- **Unlimited:** Unlimited generations in Explore Mode; 2,250 credits per month in Credit Mode for non-Explore tasks
- **Enterprise:** Custom credits, negotiated as part of the contract

Credits generally translate into seconds of video or number of images. For example, approximately five credits correspond to one second of Gen-2 video. Therefore, Standard’s 625 credits can yield roughly 125 seconds of Gen-2 content per month.

### Feature Unlocks by Plan

- **Watermark Removal & Upscaling:**
- Basic: No watermark removal or upscaling
- Standard & Above: Upscaling for Gen-1/Gen-2; watermark removal for Gen-1, Gen-2, and Gen-3 Alpha .
- **Model Access (Generations):**
- Basic: Gen-1 (up to 4 sec), Gen-2 (up to 16 sec via Extend Video)
- Standard: Gen-1 up to 15 sec; Gen-2 up to 16 sec; Gen-3 Alpha up to 10 sec
- Pro & Unlimited: All Gen-1/Gen-2/Gen-3 Alpha capabilities from Standard, plus higher throughput in Explore Mode (Unlimited).
- **Storage & Projects:**
- Basic: 3 projects, 5 GB storage
- Standard: Unlimited projects, 100 GB storage
- Pro: Unlimited projects, 500 GB storage
- Unlimited & Enterprise: Essentially unlimited segmentation via teamspaces and custom storage (Enterprise) .
- **Exports & Formats:**
- Basic: 720p video exports, limited image formats
- Standard: 4K video exports, green screen alpha matte, 2K images, full 3D textures
- Pro: All Standard exports plus PNG & ProRes for videos and images .
- **Custom AI & Voice Tools:**
- Standard & Above: One custom AI generator training included
- Pro & Above: Ability to create custom voices for lip sync and TTS .

### Integration and API Access

While Basic users rely on the web interface, all paid tiers provide API access, allowing seamless integration with workflows (e.g., Adobe Creative Suite, Canva, Figma). Enterprise accounts receive advanced API support and integration consulting, ensuring the platform aligns with internal toolchains.

## How Can Users Optimize Their Costs?

Choosing the right plan and managing credits effectively can substantially impact a user’s overall expenditure. Below are strategies for cost optimization within Runway AI’s 2025 framework.

### Annual vs. Monthly Billing

Runway offers discounts for annual commitments across Standard, Pro, and Unlimited tiers:

- **Standard:** $12/user/month when billed annually (vs. $15/month)
- **Pro:** $28/user/month when billed annually (vs. $35/month)
- **Unlimited:** $76/user/month when billed annually (vs. $95/month)

This translates to a 20% savings on Standard ($144 vs. $180) and similar reductions for Pro and Unlimited users. Organizations with predictable usage patterns and stable teams can reduce costs by choosing annual billing .

### Credit Management

- **Estimate Monthly Needs:** Users should track historical credit consumption (e.g., how many seconds of Gen-2 or Gen-3 content they generate) to choose the smallest adequate tier.
- **Top-Up Credits:** If users occasionally exceed their tier’s monthly credits, they can purchase additional credits at Runway’s pay-as-you-go rates—often more cost-effective than upgrading plans temporarily.
- **Shift Between Explore and Credit Modes:** Unlimited tier users should exploit Explore Mode for large-scale experimentation (unlimited at relaxed rates) and reserve credit mode for final, high-quality renders.

### Team Seats and Seat Rotation

For teams where not all members need equal access, consider rotating seats:

- Organizations can assign seats to core users (e.g., lead editor, pipeline architect) who use Runway heavily, while occasional contributors operate under lighter usage or rotate into paid seats only when needed.
- Seat rotation ensures that only the necessary number of active users incur subscription costs—particularly useful in the Standard or Pro tiers where each seat subscribes to a fixed cost .

### Annual Commitments with Conserved Credits

Enterprise clients can negotiate custom credit bundles that roll over unused credits for a specified period, reducing waste. Large customers often secure multi-year deals at locked rates, effectively hedging against future price increases .

## What Are the Enterprise Plan’s Customization Options?

The Enterprise plan transcends straightforward subscription tiers by offering bespoke solutions tailored to large-scale, security-sensitive environments. Key highlights include:

### Single Sign-On (SSO) and Security Compliance

- **SSO Integration:** Enterprise accounts can integrate with common identity providers (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace), streamlining user management and access control.
- **Advanced Compliance:** Features such as SOC 2 Type II certification, GDPR adherence, and optional on-premise or VPC deployment ensure that sensitive data remains secure and compliant with industry regulations .

### Custom Credit and Storage Allocations

- **Flexible Credit Structures:** Enterprises negotiate credit volumes (monthly or annual) tailored to project pipelines. Unused credits can sometimes roll over within the contract period, reducing surcharges.
- **Scalable Storage Options:** Beyond the standard 500 GB offered in Pro, Enterprise accounts can secure terabyte-scale or petabyte-scale storage with bespoke pricing models—ideal for studios archiving large video libraries .

### Model Customizations and Integrations

- **Bespoke AI Models:** Enterprises can request model fine-tuning or white-label versions of Gen-n models with proprietary training datasets for brand-specific aesthetics or compliance requirements.
- **API & Workflow Integrations:** Dedicated API endpoints, webhooks, and SFTP integration allow Runway to plug directly into an organization’s existing pipeline—be it editing suites, CDN deployments, or data lakes.
- **Configurable Teamspaces:** Administrators can create segmented teamspaces to isolate departments (e.g., marketing, post-production, R&D), each with unique permissions, asset policies, and usage dashboards .

### Enterprise-Wide Onboarding and Support

- **Dedicated Success Team:** Runway’s Enterprise customers receive white-glove onboarding, including initial workshops, ongoing training sessions, and a dedicated account manager.
- **Priority Support:** 24/7 support channels with guaranteed SLA response times ensure minimal downtime, crucial for production schedules.
- **Workspace Analytics:** Real-time dashboards track seat usage, credit consumption, and project milestones—allowing management to optimize workflows and budgets proactively.

## How Does Runway AI’s 2025 Pricing Compare to Competitors?

In 2025, the AI video-generation market has matured, with several direct and adjacent competitors offering similar services. Below is a brief comparison of Runway AI’s pricing relative to key rivals—Synthesia, Pika Labs, and Meta’s generative AI services. While exact feature sets and credit mechanics vary, this section provides a high-level overview to contextualize value.

### Synthesia

- **Pricing:** Starts at $30/user/month for basic text-to-video avatars; enterprise tier at $120/user/month for custom avatars and advanced branding.
- **Features:** Primarily focuses on AI-driven avatar creation and localized voiceovers; limited customization for video backgrounds.
- **Comparison:** Runway’s Standard ($15/mo) and Pro ($35/mo) plans offer broader visual generation (Gen-1, Gen-2, Gen-3) and higher resolution exports compared to Synthesia’s avatar-centric capabilities. Synthesia is often chosen for quick, template-based corporate videos, while Runway suits creative content requiring deeper editing and custom generative flexibility.

### Pika Labs

- **Pricing:** Meta’s professional video-generation API is priced at $0.10 per second of 4K output (non-subscription model) with volume discounts above 10,000 seconds per month.
- **Features:** Robust text-to-video with advanced inpainting but requires significant technical integration and per-use billing.
- **Comparison:** While Meta’s per-second billing can be economical for sporadic high-resolution outputs, Runway’s all-you-can-generate Explore Mode (Unlimited plan at $95/mo) often yields lower predictable costs for high-frequency content production. Enterprises with steady pipelines might prefer the subscription predictability and integrated editing suite that Runway offers .
