# Muse Wonder vs GPT-6.1 Sol: Benchmarks, Pricing, Speed, and Suitable Cases

![Muse Wonder vs GPT-6.1 Sol: Benchmarks, Pricing, Speed, and Suitable Cases](https://resource.cometapi.com/11a%E4%B8%8B%E8%BD%BD.png)

**TL;DR:** Meta’s Muse Wonder and OpenAI’s GPT-6.1 Sol are advanced reasoning models for coding, professional workflows, and AI agents. Both offer approximately one million tokens of context, but differ in model design, output speed, and usage costs.

[Artificial Analysis reports](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/comparisons/muse-wonder-vs-gpt-6-1-sol) an Intelligence Index score of 52 for both models in its Max comparison, checked on October 15, 2026. Muse Wonder leads on selected business and automation evaluations; GPT-6.1 Sol leads on selected terminal-coding and long-context tests. These are independent third-party results, not tests conducted for this article.

According to the dated pricing sources cited in this article, the listed base rates are $2/$10 per million input/output tokens for GPT-6.1 Sol and $4/$20 for Muse Wonder. These figures should be checked against the official provider documentation before production use. This makes GPT-6.1 Sol’s base rates 50% lower, but its long-context surcharges change that comparison. Choose using measured task success, latency and total billable usage, rather than token prices alone.
