# Tencent Hyra 2.0: Harness Architecture, Benchmarks & Results

![Tencent Hyra 2.0: Harness Architecture, Benchmarks & Results](https://resource.cometapi.com/console/blog-banner/2026/11/448c6a2524d741bcb2baeb75c0df7e5f.jpeg)

## TL;DR

Tencent Hunyuan introduced Hyra 2.0 on November 2, 2026 as a research Harness for developing scientific models, software tools, and reusable workflows. Its defining feature is persistent coordination across multi-day or multi-week experiments.

The public release currently consists of selected research artifacts—models, code, weights, and reports—rather than a generally available hosted Hyra 2.0 service. As of November 4, 2026, Tencent’s public materials do not document a general-purpose orchestration API or subscription plan.

## Key Takeaways

- Research objective: Hyra 2.0 develops reusable scientific models and tools that can support more than one research project.
- Persistent coordination: Overall, Mission, and Worker agents divide responsibilities, while queues and research records preserve progress across long experiments.
- Reusable knowledge: Agent memory, Books, and research skills retain findings together with their supporting evidence and conditions of use.
