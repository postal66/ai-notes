# Gemini 2.5 Flash Image launched— the feature-rich image model is live in CometAPI

![Gemini 2.5 Flash Image launched— the feature-rich image model is live in CometAPI](https://resource.cometapi.com/blog/uploads/2025/08/gemini-2.5-flash.webp)

Google lately unveiled *Gemini 2.5 Flash Image* — a native, high-performance image generation and editing model that brings real-time, conversational image creation and precise, multi-step editing directly into the Gemini product family and developer tools. The release, described by Google as a “state-of-the-art” update to Gemini’s multimodal stack, is positioned for both consumer creativity and production-grade developer workflows.

Gemini 2.5 Flash Image combines generation, in-place editing and multi-turn conversational control. Users can prompt the model with text, upload an image to be edited, and iterate in subsequent turns while preserving contextual constraints (for example, consistent likeness in portrait edits). Google emphasizes finer control—style transfer, outfit or background swaps, blending of separate photos and more—aimed at both content creators and teams that need repeatable, reliable image edits.

## What makes it stand out

- **Conversational multi-turn editing** — keep refining an image by following a natural back-and-forth rather than rebuilding from scratch.
- **Likeness and consistency controls** — Google highlights safeguards and modelling improvements to keep edits faithful to the source subject (useful for portraits and pet photos). Maintain the visual identity of recurring characters across frames and edits for storytelling and branding use cases.
- **Low-latency “Flash” family performance** — Built on the 2.5 Flash family, the image model is tuned for low latency and efficient inference to suit interactive and high-throughput scenarios. Google describes 2.5 Flash Image as delivering a balance of quality, speed, and cost for developers.
- **Blend and composition tools:** Combine multiple inputs into a single coherent image while preserving photographic or illustrative detail.

On safety and provenance, Google said images generated or edited with Gemini 2.5 Flash Image will carry an **invisibleSynthID digital watermark**, designed to allow later identification of AI-generated content.The per-image pricing (**$30 per 1,000,000 output tokens** for Gemini 2.5 Flash Image. Google states a standard 1024×1024 output image consumes **1,290 output tokens**,Google’s example at ~$0.039 per image) places the model in a competitive band for production use—especially where volume, speed and multi-image fusion are priorities—while the watermarking and Studio tooling address adoption and compliance concerns.
