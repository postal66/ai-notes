# How to Make ChatGPT Sound more human through Prompt

![How to Make ChatGPT Sound more human through Prompt](https://resource.cometapi.com/blog/uploads/2025/06/How-to-Make-ChatGPT-Sound-more-human-through-Prompt.webp)

As AI systems like ChatGPT become integral to customer service, content creation, and personal assistance, users demand interactions that feel natural, empathetic, and personalized. Recent updates—such as ChatGPT’s “Custom Instructions” feature and enhancements to its Advanced Voice Mode—empower users to fine-tune the AI’s tone, style, and emotional expressiveness. Understanding these tools, alongside proven prompt-engineering techniques, is key to unlocking a more human-like conversational experience.

## How can Custom Instructions personalize ChatGPT’s tone and style?

### Understanding Custom Instructions

OpenAI’s Custom Instructions allow users to define specific guidelines about their preferences and context, which ChatGPT then incorporates into every response. By specifying details such as desired tone, role, and formatting rules, you ensure consistency and relevance in the AI’s outputs. For instance, a marketing manager might instruct ChatGPT to “write in a friendly, enthusiastic tone with bullet points for key features,” leading to responses that match brand voice and audience expectations.

### Setting Up Effective Custom Instructions

1. **Identify Your Audience and Purpose**: Clearly state who the readers are and what you want to achieve—for example, “Explain technical concepts to non-technical users in simple, conversational language.”
2. **Define Tone and Formality Level**: Use descriptors like “warm,” “empathetic,” or “professional” to guide ChatGPT’s stylistic choices.
3. **Specify Structural Preferences**: Indicate whether you prefer lists, headings, or concise paragraphs.
4. **Iterate and Refine**: Review the AI’s outputs and adjust your instructions to close any remaining gap between expectation and reality.

## What enhancements does Advanced Voice Mode bring to natural interactions?

### Advanced Voice Mode Improvements

OpenAI’s recent update to ChatGPT’s Advanced Voice Mode introduces subtler intonation, natural pauses, and improved emotional expressiveness, making spoken responses feel more engaging and less robotic . These refinements allow the AI to convey empathy, sarcasm, and enthusiasm more accurately—traits essential for creating rapport in voice-based applications such as virtual assistants or customer support systems.

### Ethical and Practical Considerations

While enhanced voice capabilities offer a richer user experience, they raise questions about authenticity and consent. OpenAI’s decision to retire certain celebrity-like voices—such as the previously available “Sky” voice—underscores the importance of avoiding unauthorized mimicry and respecting actor rights . When integrating voice features, always disclose that users are interacting with an AI and choose voice profiles that align with your ethical guidelines.

## How can prompt engineering adjust tone, style, and emotional depth?

### Crafting Conversational Tone Prompts

To achieve an informal, friendly style, include explicit instructions in your prompt:

> “Write this explanation as if you’re talking to a friend—use short sentences, casual connectors like ‘so’ or ‘well,’ and sprinkle in questions such as ‘What do you think?’” .
> This approach harnesses burstiness—variation in sentence length and complexity—to mimic human speech patterns and maintain reader engagement.

### Eliciting Empathy and Emotion

Prompting ChatGPT to incorporate emotional cues can significantly enhance perceived authenticity. For example:

> “Describe this customer service scenario and focus on conveying empathy—use phrases like ‘I understand how frustrating this can be’ and ‘It must have been challenging for you.’” .
> By embedding empathy instructions, you guide the model to select emotionally resonant language, fostering a deeper connection with the reader.

## What narrative techniques can make AI responses more relatable?

### Leveraging Storytelling and Personal Anecdotes

Humans connect with stories. Instruct ChatGPT to share brief, illustrative anecdotes or hypothetical scenarios:

> “Explain financial planning principles by telling a short story about someone saving for their first home.” .
> This narrative framing contextualizes information, making it memorable and engaging.

### Incorporating Rhetorical Questions and Casual Transitions

Using rhetorical questions invites readers into the dialogue. Example prompt:

> “Introduce the topic with a question like ‘Have you ever wondered why…?’ and use transitions like ‘Anyway,’ ‘By the way,’ or ‘Now, here’s the catch.’”.
> Casual transitions simulate the ebb and flow of natural speech, breaking up formal monotony and keeping the conversation dynamic.

## What best practices ensure consistency, authenticity, and ongoing improvement?

### Iterative Prompt Refinement

1. **A/B Test Variations**: Compare different prompt phrasings to identify which yields the most human-like response.
2. **Analyze Metrics**: Measure engagement metrics—such as read time or user feedback—to gauge effectiveness.
3. **Adjust Based on Context**: Tailor prompts for specific use cases (e.g., technical documentation vs. social media posts).

### Testing Across Multiple Contexts

Ensure your prompts hold up across a range of topics and interaction styles. For each context—be it educational, casual, or professional—fine-tune instructions to maintain voice consistency without sacrificing authenticity.
