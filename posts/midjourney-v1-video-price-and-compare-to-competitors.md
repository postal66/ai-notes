# Midjourney V1 video: Price and Compare to Competitors

![Midjourney V1 video: Price and Compare to Competitors](https://resource.cometapi.com/blog/uploads/2025/06/midjourney002.webp)

Midjourney’s introduction of its first video generation model,Midjourney V1 Video (V1), marks a pivotal moment in the evolution of AI-driven creativity. By enabling users to animate still images into 5‑second video clips, Midjourney bridges the gap between static visual art and dynamic storytelling. Below is an in‑depth, professionally structured exploration of Midjourney V1 Video—covering its features, pricing, trial options, limitations, and what lies ahead.

## What is Midjourney V1 Video?

Midjourney V1 Video represents the platform’s first step into AI‑driven video generation. Unlike purely text‑to‑video systems, V1 employs an **Image‑to‑Video** workflow, leveraging existing Midjourney images as “starting frames” and animating them into short clips.

### What capabilities does Midjourney V1 Video offer?

- **5‑Second Baseline Clips**: Each generated video begins as a 5‑second sequence, automatically animated from a single frame. Users can extend this by up to four increments (4 seconds each), for a maximum of 21 seconds total.
- **Automatic vs. Manual Animation**:
- *Automatic Mode* generates motion via a built‑in “motion prompt” that simply “makes things move.”
- *Manual Mode* opens the prompt bar for users to craft directional cues (e.g., “camera pans right, subject rotates,” etc.) .
- **Motion Intensity Controls**: Two settings—`--motion low` (subtle, slow movements) and `--motion high` (dramatic camera or subject shifts)—allow stylistic flexibility.
- **Raw Parameter**: The `--raw` flag reduces Midjourney’s interpretive flair, giving the user’s text prompts greater influence over how motion unfolds .

### How does the Image‑to‑Video workflow work?

1. **Select or Upload a Starting Frame**: On the Midjourney website, open your gallery image and click “Animate,” or upload a new image via the Imagine bar’s image icon .
2. **Choose Motion Mode**: Opt for *Auto* or *Manual*, then specify `--motion low` or `--motion high` if desired.
3. **Generate and Review**: Midjourney processes the request—each video requires roughly 8 GPU minutes—and presents the clip for playback, scrubbing, and download.
4. **Extend if Needed**: Use “Extend Auto” or “Extend Manual” buttons to add 4 more seconds, up to four times (max 21 seconds).

## How much does Midjourney V1 Video cost?

Given that video rendering is far more computationally intensive than static image generation, Midjourney employs a clear pricing structure, integrated into its existing subscription plans.

### What are the subscription tiers?

Midjourney offers four plans—Basic, Standard, Pro, and Mega—with monthly and annual billing options. Key highlights include :

| Plan | Monthly Price | Annual Price (20% Off) | Fast GPU Time | Relax Mode (Images/Videos) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Basic** | $10 | $96 ($8/mo) | 3.3 hr (200 min) | Images only; videos in Fast Mode |
| **Standard** | $30 | $288 ($24/mo) | 15 hr | Unlimited images; videos in Fast Mode only |
| **Pro** | $60 | $576 ($48/mo) | 30 hr | Unlimited images & video in Relax Mode |
| **Mega** | $120 | $1,152 ($96/mo) | 60 hr | Unlimited images & video in Relax Mode |

*Stealth Mode (private creations) is available only on Pro and Mega.*

### How is video pricing calculated?

- **GPU Time Multiplier**: A video prompt consumes roughly *8 GPU minutes*, compared to *1 GPU minute* for an image .
- **Relative Cost**: Videos therefore “cost 8× more” GPU time than images, equating to roughly “one image’s worth of cost per second of video”.
- **Extra GPU Time**: If you exhaust your Fast GPU time before renewal, you may purchase additional GPU time at $4 per hour, or earn extra Fast time through in‑platform activities .

### Are there any discounts or trials?

- **Annual Discount**: Opting for yearly billing saves 20% on all plans.
- **Free Trials**: As of July 2025, **no free trials** are active; Midjourney has suspended trial offerings and has not announced a return date. New users must subscribe to begin video generation.

- **Ease of Use:** The familiar Discord interface and simple commands give Midjourney a usability edge for existing community members.

## What are the limitations of Midjourney V1 Video?

While a major innovation, Midjourney V1 Video has notable constraints reflective of its “stepping stone” status.

### Video quality and length

- **480p Resolution**: Standard Definition only—videos scale based on your image’s aspect ratio (e.g., 624×624 for 1:1; 832×464 for 16:9) .
- **Maximum Duration**: 21 seconds. Midjourney caps each clip at 5 seconds initially and allows up to four 4‑second extensions .

### Motion settings and control

- **No Skeletal Rigging**: V1 cannot manipulate articulated joints—animation is procedural rather than bone‑driven.
- **Glitches at High Motion**: Dramatic settings may yield unrealistic artifacts or jitter.

### Compatibility and access modes

- **Web‑only**: Video generation is *exclusively* on the Midjourney website; Discord commands (`/video`) are not supported.
- **Plan Restrictions**: Relax Mode for video (unlimited queued processing) is available only to Pro and Mega subscribers .

## Conclusion

Midjourney V1 Video is both a landmark feature and a harbinger of where AI‑driven creativity is headed. By democratizing video generation—integrating it seamlessly into an image‑centric workflow—Midjourney empowers users to explore motion, narrative, and dynamism with unprecedented ease. While limitations remain in resolution, duration, and IP compliance, V1 lays the groundwork for richer, more interactive AI experiences on the horizon.
