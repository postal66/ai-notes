# Does Suno Have an App

![Does Suno Have an App](https://resource.cometapi.com/suno-logo-2024-billboard-1548.webp)

## Quick answer

If someone is asking, “Does Suno have an app?” the answer is a clean yes. Suno currently has official app listings on the Apple App Store and Google Play, and the company also runs a web experience on Suno’s own site. The app is not just a “listen-only” companion; Suno’s official listings describe it as a creation tool for making full songs from text, voice, rhythm ideas, or audio uploads.

That matters because Suno is no longer positioned as only a novelty AI song generator. Over the last two years, Suno said nearly 100 million people have made music on the platform, and the company has continued to add more serious creation tools, including Suno Studio and v5.5.

## What Suno is

Suno is an AI music generation platform that turns prompts into songs. On its official App Store and Google Play pages, Suno says users can make music, beats, and lyrics with AI, even if they have no prior experience. The company’s site also describes Suno as a place to create and share original music quickly from simple prompts.

That “create from a prompt” idea is the core of Suno’s appeal. But the platform has steadily broadened beyond basic prompt-to-song generation. Suno now includes mobile creation, a browser-based studio workflow, and new personalization features that aim to make the output feel less generic and more like the user.

### Latest Suno news: what changed most recently

Suno’s most recent headline-level update is the March 26, 2026 release of v5.5. According to Suno’s help documentation, v5.5 is its “most expressive” model yet and introduces Voices, Custom Models, and My Taste. Voices is meant to capture the essence of the user’s voice, Custom Models let Pro and Premier users create personalized variants, and My Taste adapts to a user’s preferences.

A second major update came earlier in 2026 with Suno Studio 1.2. Suno says the Studio update added Warp Markers, Remove FX, Alternates, and Time Signature support, making the workstation more useful for creators who want more control over production details. That is a meaningful signal: Suno is not just chasing viral, instant songs anymore; it is building a fuller creative environment.

## Yes, Suno has an app — here are the official options

### 1) iPhone app

Suno launched its mobile app in July 2024 and initially rolled it out on iOS in the United States, with Android and global rollout plans mentioned by the company at the time. Today, Suno’s App Store listing is live and describes the app as a full music-creation tool that supports text prompts, humming, tapping a beat, and audio uploads.

For users, the biggest value of the iPhone app is convenience. It puts the creative workflow in your pocket, which means you can capture an idea instantly instead of waiting until you are back at a desktop. Suno’s own launch post explicitly framed the app as something meant for “every day” inspiration, not just a formal studio session.

### 2) Android app

Suno also has an official Android listing on Google Play. The Play Store description mirrors the App Store positioning: AI music generation, text prompts, humming, tapping rhythms, and uploading audio. That means the mobile experience is now cross-platform, not iOS-only.

This is a big deal for reach. A lot of creative apps launch on one platform and stay there for months or years, but Suno’s presence on both major mobile ecosystems makes it much easier for casual users, creators, and teams to test it quickly.

### 3) Web app

Suno is also available on the web through its own site, where users can start creating music directly in the browser. Suno’s homepage and explore pages position the web experience as a fast way to generate original tracks and discover music from other creators.

For serious users, the browser experience still matters a lot. Mobile is great for quick inspiration, but the web is usually better for longer sessions, more precise editing, and more comfortable screen real estate. Suno’s broader push into Studio makes that desktop-oriented workflow even more relevant.

## Key Features of the Suno Mobile Apps

The apps deliver a powerful generative audio workstation in your pocket:

- **Text-to-Song Generation**: Turn prompts into full songs with lyrics, vocals, and instrumentation across genres (hip-hop, pop, rock, electronic, country, etc.).
- **Audio Input**: Hum, tap beats, upload recordings, or record your voice for custom tracks.
- **Lyrics Generation**: AI writes or refines lyrics from ideas, poems, or chat texts.
- **Suno Studio**: Edit structure, vocals, melodies, add instruments, and remix on mobile.
- **Discovery & Community**: Browse trending songs, playlists, follow creators, share, and go viral.
- **Library Sync**: Seamless across devices with account login.
- **High-Quality Output**: Studio-like results suitable for personal, social, or (with paid plans) commercial use.

Users praise on-the-go creativity: “Create complex scores in minutes with no experience.” Some note Android audio upload quirks, but overall satisfaction is high.
