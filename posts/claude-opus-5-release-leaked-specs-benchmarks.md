## What Is Claude Opus 5?

Claude Opus 5 would represent the next generational leap in Anthropic’s Opus-tier models, following the incremental 4.x series (Opus 4.8 being the latest as of May 28, 2026). The Opus line has historically delivered Anthropic’s most capable models for complex reasoning, long-context understanding, advanced coding, and agentic workflows.

For context on the evolution:

- Claude Opus 4 series focused on autonomous work (up to 7+ hours), advanced coding, and tool use.
- Recent point releases (4.6–4.8) emphasized honesty, reliability (e.g., 4x fewer overlooked code flaws in 4.8), dynamic workflows, and effort control.

Opus 5 rumors position it as a major upgrade, potentially aligning or surpassing Fable 5 in raw power while maintaining broader usability without heavy fallbacks. Leaks mention codenames like "Honeycomb" and sightings in tools like Cursor, with features such as 1M token context (standard), "xhigh" effort modes, and superior SWE (Software Engineering) benchmarks.

## Rumored Performance and Specifications

Details are speculative but drawn from consistent leaks and community analysis as of July 2026:

- **Context Window:** Expected 1M tokens standard (matching recent Opus and Sonnet models), with potential expansions.
- **Output Tokens:** Up to 128K or more, supporting long-form generation and complex agent trajectories.
- **Reasoning & Effort:** Adaptive thinking on by default, with multi-level effort controls (including xhigh) for trading compute against quality. Builds on dynamic workflows and parallel sub-agents from Opus 4.8.
- **Agentic & Coding Capabilities:** Strong emphasis on reliability (e.g., 4x fewer unremarked code flaws vs. prior versions), computer use, browser agents, and multi-step planning. Leaks claim superior SWE-Pro performance vs. GPT-5.6.
- **Multimodal:** Continued vision input; potential improvements in high-resolution or sustained visual reasoning.
- **Safety:** Enhanced constitutional AI safeguards, with possible Mythos-like classifiers for sensitive domains (cyber, bio).
- **Tokenizer:** Likely an updated version (similar to Sonnet 5’s ~30% token increase for better performance).

Supporting data from prior models shows steady gains: Opus 4.8 achieved 84% on Online-Mind2Web for browser agents and strong Terminal-Bench results. Sonnet 5 closes much of the gap at lower cost. Opus 5 is anticipated to push these further.
