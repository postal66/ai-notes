# Claude Duet Explained: What it is, how to work & Access

![Claude Duet Explained: What it is, how to work & Access](https://resource.cometapi.com/console/blog-banner/2026/10/d4e6104547484953b20ca9f69ba4da68.jpg)

## **TLDR**

On October 21, 2026, Anthropic opened a research preview of Claude Duet — a proactive, always-on AI agent app powered by Claude Opus 5.5. Unlike traditional chat or the earlier Claude Cowork mode, Duet comes to you with morning briefs, monitors connected apps, works autonomously on its own cloud computer, and only asks for approval before sensitive actions (sending messages, payments, signing, deleting).

Available via waitlist to US Pro and Max subscribers at no extra cost (shares plan limits). This guide covers what Duet is, how it works, pricing, a detailed side-by-side with Claude Cowork (now largely merged into main Claude), and when to build your own agent on the Claude API or via platforms like CometAPI instead.

### Key Takeaways

- Claude Duet is a standalone proactive agent (push-based) designed for professionals and small businesses; it initiates contact and runs in the background even when your devices are offline.
- Powered exclusively by Claude Opus 5.5; connects to 6,000+ marketplace connectors; requires explicit approval for high-impact actions.
