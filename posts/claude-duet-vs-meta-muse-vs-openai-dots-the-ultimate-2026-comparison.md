# Claude Duet vs. Meta Muse vs. OpenAI Dots: The Ultimate 2026 Comparison

![Claude Duet vs. Meta Muse vs. OpenAI Dots: The Ultimate 2026 Comparison](https://resource.cometapi.com/console/blog-banner/2026/11/fd069aa81548426a8b65298a9476c821.png)

**TLDR**
In late 2026, three major players launched proactive “always-on” AI agents: Anthropic’s Claude Duet (October 21), Meta’s Muse (September 8), and OpenAI’s Dots (September 29). All three run on dedicated cloud computers with browsers, connect to apps, handle multi-step tasks, and seek approval for sensitive actions.

Claude Duet emphasizes professional and small-business workflows with morning briefs and strict approvals. Meta Muse targets everyday personal life (with a free tier) and WhatsApp integration. OpenAI Dots focus on persistent goals inside ChatGPT, Slack, and Teams, with customizable rules. Choice depends on your region, existing subscriptions, budget, and whether you prioritize work productivity, personal errands, or enterprise/team use. Developers building on top of these models can access Claude, GPT-series, and related models cost-effectively through unified platforms like CometAPI.
