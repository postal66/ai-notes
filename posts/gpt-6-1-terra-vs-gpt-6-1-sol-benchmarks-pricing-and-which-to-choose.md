# GPT-6.1 Terra vs GPT-6.1 Sol: Benchmarks, Pricing, and Which to Choose

![GPT-6.1 Terra vs GPT-6.1 Sol: Benchmarks, Pricing, and Which to Choose](https://resource.cometapi.com/console/blog-banner/2026/10/15b426445ddf46d2ba02564120e672a7.png)

## TL;DR

Start with GPT-6.1 Terra for high-volume, verifiable, cost-sensitive tasks. Evaluate GPT-6.1 Sol for complex coding, multi-step tool use, and workflows where failures are expensive. A practical default is to use Terra first and escalate to Sol when predefined tests or validation checks fail.

## Key Takeaways

- **Standard token pricing:** On OpenAI Standard, Terra costs $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens; Sol costs $2 and $10, respectively.
- **Task-specific performance:** At maximum reasoning effort, Terra narrowly leads on Agents’ Last Exam v1 and Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1, while Sol leads on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and OSWorld 2.1.
- **Shared context capacity:** Both models offer a 1.05-million-token context window and accept text and image inputs.
