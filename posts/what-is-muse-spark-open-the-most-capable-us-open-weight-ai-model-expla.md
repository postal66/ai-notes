# What Is Muse Spark Open? The Most Capable US Open-Weight AI Model Explained

![What Is Muse Spark Open? The Most Capable US Open-Weight AI Model Explained](https://resource.cometapi.com/1212%E4%B8%8B%E8%BD%BD.webp)

**TL;DR:** Meta released [Muse Spark Open](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open) on October 15, 2026, introducing an open-weight multimodal model designed for coding, tool use, and autonomous agent workflows. With approximately 816 billion total parameters, 42 billion active parameters per token, and up to one million tokens of context, the release brings large-scale MoE capabilities to downloadable model weights and hosted inference services.

The core trade-off: you gain deployment and customization control, but you take on hardware, licensing, and evaluation costs. Meta calls it the most capable US open-weight model. That claim does not mean it ranks first on every global benchmark.

## Key Takeaways

- Muse Spark Open uses a sparse Mixture-of-Experts architecture with approximately 816B total parameters and 42B active parameters per token, and supports up to 1M tokens of context.
- Muse Spark Open accepts text, image, audio, and video inputs, and generates text outputs.
