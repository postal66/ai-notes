## TL;DR

GPT-6.1 Terra and Claude Haiku 5.5 offer different ways to reduce the cost of useful AI work. Terra is the stronger starting point for demanding automation and terminal coding in the independent tests discussed below. Haiku offers substantially lower token prices and competitive professional knowledge-work performance.

For prompts up to 100K tokens, official standard API prices are $1 input / $5 output per million tokens for Terra and $0.10 / $0.50 for Haiku. Those rates make Haiku 90% cheaper per token, but reasoning, retries, caching, and long-context tiers determine what a completed task actually costs. Choose against a representative workload, rather than a single headline score.
