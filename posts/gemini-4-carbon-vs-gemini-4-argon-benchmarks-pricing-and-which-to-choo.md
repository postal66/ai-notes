# Gemini 4 Carbon vs Gemini 4 Argon: Benchmarks, Pricing, and Which to Choose

![Gemini 4 Carbon vs Gemini 4 Argon: Benchmarks, Pricing, and Which to Choose](https://resource.cometapi.com/console/blog-banner/2026/10/4b673910bc354c44bd3dad46761813b5.png)

## TL;DR

Google announced [Gemini 4 Carbon](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/) on October 28, 2026, with stronger published results than Gemini 4 Argon across coding, long-context reasoning, and cybersecurity. For developers, the immediate choice depends on access: Argon is available through the paid Gemini API, while Carbon remains in a restricted rollout. This comparison reviews their benchmarks, API specifications, speed, and pricing to help you decide what to deploy now and when to evaluate Carbon.

## Key Takeaways

- **Performance:** Carbon scores 59 versus Argon’s 50 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0. Gains vary by benchmark: SWE-sweep improves by 5.3 percentage points, while GraphWalks BFS F1 improves by 12.0 points.
- Argon documents a unified 1,048,576-token window shared by input, thinking, and output. Carbon’s public API limits and tool support have not been announced.
