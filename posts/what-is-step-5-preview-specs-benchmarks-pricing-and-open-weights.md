# What Is Step 5 Preview? Specs, Benchmarks, Pricing, and Open Weights

![What Is Step 5 Preview? Specs, Benchmarks, Pricing, and Open Weights](https://resource.cometapi.com/console/blog-banner/2026/10/cb2e500a2b8b4da4a73f9d047269f82f.png)

## TL;DR

Step 5 Preview is StepFun’s flagship AI model for software engineering, financial research, and complex agent workflows. It combines competitive task performance with cost-efficient inference, although its results vary across benchmarks. Developers can access it through a hosted API or deploy its open weights, depending on their budget, infrastructure, and performance requirements.

## Key Takeaways

- **Architecture:** Step 5 Preview uses a sparse MoE architecture with 600B total parameters and 27B active per token, balancing model capacity and inference efficiency.
- **Context and multimodal support:** The model supports a 1M-token context window, text, image, and video inputs, and up to 64K output tokens through its hosted API.
- Step 5 Preview achieves 67.7% on DeepSWE v1.1 and 66.4% on FrontierFinance, although it trails several competitors on Terminal-Bench v4.
