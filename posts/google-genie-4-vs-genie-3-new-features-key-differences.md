# Google Genie 4 vs Genie 3: New Features & Key Differences

![Google Genie 4 vs Genie 3: New Features & Key Differences](https://resource.cometapi.com/console/blog-banner/2026/10/f9cb7e5177bd421e83321df010f36b0f.jpg)

## TL;DR

Google DeepMind announced [Genie 4 on October 27, 2026](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/). Its new world model adds native audio, interactive character dialogue, and Gemini-assisted scene control to the real-time visual environments established by Genie 3.

For developers, designers, and researchers, Genie 4 opens up richer ways to prototype a scene: you can explore an environment, change what happens, and hear characters respond. Its strongest appeal is interactive storytelling and early concept development. Access begins with a restricted research preview.

## Key Takeaways

- **The main upgrade:** Genie 4 adds sound, responsive characters, and more expressive scene control to the visual worlds established by Genie 3.
- Genie 4 combines contextual scene steering with Gemini-assisted planning for sequences of events.
